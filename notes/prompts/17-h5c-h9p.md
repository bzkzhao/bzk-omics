**H5c, which has no family run.**
1. **Durability** comes from the committed
   `tests/fixtures/pxd026748_reconstruction.json`: the per-claim `min_p` over
   its 360 members. A claim is durable if P < 0.01 in all 360.
   - **Verify first** that recomputing the categories gives the fixture's
     111 durable and 177 underdetermined. If not, stop.
   - **Never re-run the family** for H5c.
2. **The flag** is Supplementary Table 1's `In vivo ISG15 targets` column, read
   from the pinned supplement (`sha256:872371eb…36a870`, sheet `Table 1`, header
   located by content). A cell is **flagged** if its value is `x`. Report every
   distinct cell value you saw.
3. **The join** is on the published row number: Table 1's `#` against the
   fixture claim's `published_row`. Any claim that fails to join stops the run.
4. **The population** is claims in clusters `Cluster 1a`, `Cluster 1b` and
   `Cluster 2`. Cluster 3 is excluded, as v6 says. Report the group sizes.
5. **The measure** is each group's durable proportion, and their difference,
   flagged minus unflagged.
6. **The verdict** is computed mechanically:
   - difference ≥ +0.10: `discriminates`;
   - difference ≤ −0.10: `inverted`;
   - otherwise `does_not_discriminate`.

   The thresholds are **inclusive** at ±0.10. Record that choice.
7. **The declared confound, descriptive only.** Each claim's count of missing
   values in its GG row, after the pipeline's log2 and before imputation, out of
   12. It is read from the GG deposit through the same population and pipeline
   turn 16 used. Report the distribution by group. **It must not enter the
   verdict.**

**H9p, the protein-grain family.**
1. **Exposure** is Table 2's IDs, read at run time, joined to the shotgun
   matrix by turn 16's recorded rule (first entry of `Majority protein IDs`),
   and passing the shotgun filters so they reach the test. Report the
   unmatched count and the count not reaching the test.
2. **The family** is the same 360 members, run over the shotgun arm's filtered,
   normalised LFQ matrix. Support is the smallest ANOVA P value < 0.01, exactly
   as in turn 16.
3. **Per exposure protein,** record its support count and its missing-value
   count before imputation.
4. **U_p** is the underdetermined proportion (supported in some members but not
   all) **among exposure proteins with at least one missing value.**
5. **U_s** is the same proportion among the 288 site claims with at least one
   missing value. The support counts come from the committed site fixture, and
   the missingness from the GG deposit rows, as in H5c's point 7.
6. **The verdict** is computed mechanically:
   - U_p ≤ 0.5 × U_s: `particular_to_site_data`;
   - U_p ≥ 0.8 × U_s: `general_to_imputation`;
   - otherwise `indeterminate`.

   If U_s's denominator or U_p's denominator is zero, the verdict is
   `undefined`, with the reason given.
7. **Also report, descriptively:** the unconditional underdetermined proportions
   at both grains, and the counts of complete-row claims at both grains. These
   are **reported beside the verdict and never enter it.**
