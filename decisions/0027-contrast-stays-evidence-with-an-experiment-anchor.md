## Review

Landed `Proposed` at `b8ac9c7` and reviewed in the following turn. Four findings; one required no
change, one produced a `ONTOLOGY.md` split recorded below, one corrected a ground in this record, and
one is a scope note that belongs in `ROADMAP.md` rather than here. **No finding identified a defect in
the decision**, and after the correction to ground 2 it stands on two independent sufficient grounds
rather than three — which is the ground for the status above.

### §1's diagram is not a census, and this is established by counting

`Contrast` is absent from §1's partition diagram, and the review asked whether it should be added.
**It should not, and the reason is that the diagram names 19 of the 24 node tables in the DDL.**
Five are absent: `Contrast`, `EnzymeAssociation`, `Imputation`, `ProteinAssignment` and
`ProteinObservation` — the last an observation node carrying a cross-boundary edge.

**So the diagram is a schematic of the central distinction, not an enumeration of the model.** Adding
only `Contrast` would be worse than leaving it: it would make the diagram read as a census while four
node types stayed missing. Bringing all five in is a change to §1 with its own reasoning, not a review
edit to this record, and it is not made here.

### The orphan half is a different question and is now Q13

Q1's strike and its surviving orphan paragraph were left unreconciled. **They are reconciled by
splitting, not by weakening the word.** l.1077's behaviour is about I6 retraction propagation — does
retracting a curation retract the `Contrast`s it defined — and not about which node set `Contrast`
belongs to. The two were entangled only because Q1 raised the orphan as a symptom of the placement.

**`ONTOLOGY.md` §11 gains Q13**, and Q1's entry now states that its strike covers the placement
question and nothing else. **This decision changes the shape of the orphan fact without closing it**:
under an `Experiment` anchor the survivor hangs off its `Experiment` rather than off nothing, so it is
reachable and attributable, and it is still not retracted.

## Scope

Settles `ONTOLOGY.md` §11 Q1 — `Contrast`'s placement across the reference/evidence boundary — and
nothing else. **§11's questions 2, 3, 4 and 5 stay open.** No schema, DDL, identity field, edge,
invariant or datum changes here; every implied change is described under its own heading and not
made.

## Context

Q1 has two halves and they are not the same problem.

**The smell, at §11 l.1077.** If one dataset's curation is retracted, its `DifferentialResult`s are
correctly retracted through `RESULT_IN_CONTRAST` while the `Contrast` node survives, partly
orphaned.

**The collision, at l.1081, and it is the operative half.** `Contrast` identity is `numerator` +
`denominator` with **no anchor at all** — confirmed at `schema.py` l.274, `Identity(fields=
("numerator", "denominator"))` with an empty anchor tuple. The shipped strings are
`'USP18-/- + IFN'` against `'WT + IFN'`, which encode genotype and treatment and **not cell line or
organism**. A HAP1 contrast and an HCT116 contrast therefore mint one id, and `RESULT_IN_CONTRAST`
can no longer separate the two experiments' results. **It fires on correct usage.**

Two facts about the current state, both measured rather than assumed:

- **`Contrast` appears in neither column of §1's partition diagram at l.29–41.** Q1's opening
  sentence says it *"is currently an evidence node"*; §1 does not say so, and §3's row at l.119
  records the placement as unsettled. The node has been outside the partition, not on one side of it.
- **`RESULT_IN_CONTRAST` is `Contrast`'s only edge.** Grepped across `schema.py` and the DDL: l.593
  and `ONTOLOGY.md` l.508 declare it `FROM DifferentialResult TO Contrast, MANY_ONE`, and nothing
  else touches the node.

## The option list, and what the enumeration rests on

l.1079 names two. **They are not the list, and the enumeration below is not a brainstorm** — it is
generated by three axes the repository already fixes: §1 places a node by *origin*, §3 fixes
identity by *fields* and by *anchors* (the two levers, and ADR-0025 established the second), and
doing nothing is always available. Those axes generate six.

**(i) `Contrast` strictly per-dataset, duplication accepted** — l.1079's first.

**(ii) Promote to a reference node with a deterministic key over condition terms** — l.1079's
second.

**(iii) `Contrast` stays evidence and gains an anchor.** **l.1079 was short, and short in a specific
way: it enumerated *outcomes* and left the *mechanism* unnamed.** (iii) is a family; its
`Dataset`-anchored member is extensionally (i). It does not collapse into (i), because its
`Experiment`- and `Project`-anchored members are not "per-dataset" and scope differently. The
mechanism matters because ADR-0021 forbids contingent identifying fields, so *per-dataset* expressed
as a field and *per-dataset* expressed as an anchor are different changes with different costs.

**(iv) Leave it unsettled and accept the collision.** Named because it is always available.

**(v) Require `numerator` and `denominator` to be fully qualified**, carrying cell line and organism
in the strings.

**(vi) Split the node** — a reference-side definition of the condition pair and an evidence-side
per-experiment instance.

## Decision

**`Contrast` remains an evidence node, and the experimental context missing from its two strings
enters its id through an anchor on `Experiment`.** That is (iii), at `Experiment` granularity.

**Why `Experiment` and not `Dataset`, argued from the identity map rather than from convenience.**
l.1081's diagnosis is that *"any experimental context not spelled into those two strings is invisible
to the id"*. The node that holds experimental context is `Experiment`: its identity is
`("title", "modality", "organism_taxid")`, so **the anchor supplies `organism_taxid` directly**,
which is one of the two things l.1081 names as missing. `Dataset`'s identity is `("content_hash",)`
with no anchors — **a `Dataset` is one file**, so a `Dataset` anchor would separate HAP1 from HCT116
by accident of file identity while also splitting one experiment's GlyGly and matching-proteome
runs into two contrasts. `Sample` already anchors on `Experiment`, and a contrast is a comparison
between samples, so `Experiment` is the scope the samples themselves are scoped to.

**The disjointness clause, on the branch this decision takes.** §1 l.20 declares the two node sets
disjoint, and `Contrast` stays wholly inside evidence, so the partition is not crossed and
`RESULT_IN_CONTRAST` remains an evidence→evidence edge. What §11 Q1 called *"reference-like in
behaviour while evidence-like in origin"* is resolved by removing the behaviour rather than
relocating the origin: once the id carries its experiment, the node stops being reused across
experiments and the reference-like behaviour is gone. **`Contrast` also gains a column in §1's
diagram it has never had — on the evidence side.**

**How the collision is contained.** It is not tolerated. Two experiments defining `'USP18-/- + IFN'`
against `'WT + IFN'` mint two ids, because the two `Experiment` ids differ.

**What is not fixed, stated because the decision does not reach it.** l.1077's orphan smell
survives. Under an `Experiment` anchor a retracted curation still leaves the `Contrast` standing,
now hanging off the `Experiment` rather than off nothing. **This record closes l.1081 and leaves
l.1077 open inside a question it declares settled**, which is a narrowing worth naming rather than
glossing.

## Why the other five are rejected

**(ii) Promotion to reference — rejected on three independent grounds, any one sufficient.**

1. **§1 l.22 defines reference nodes as *"imported from external authorities and … never authored
   locally."*** `numerator` and `denominator` are authored locally: the curator writes them into
   `contrasts_of_interest`, and `curation_PXD018299.json` carries them as free strings. The node
   fails the definition outright.
2. ~~**§1 l.26: *"The two sets are joined only through observation nodes"*** — promoting
   `Contrast` would create a third such join and punch a hole in the constraint.~~ **Withdrawn as an
   independent ground at review, 2026-08-18, because the premise is false.** Counted against the
   shipped DDL: there are **eight** reference/evidence edges and only **two** run from an observation
   node — `MEASURED_AT` from `SiteObservation` and `RESOLVES_TO_PROTEIN` from `ProteinObservation`.
   The other six run from `Analysis` (`CURATION_CITES`), `ModifierAssignment` (`ASSIGNS`,
   `ASSIGNMENT_CITES`), `EnzymeAssociation` (`ASSOCIATION_ENZYME`, `ASSOCIATION_CITES`) and
   `ProteinAssignment` (`ASSIGNS_PROTEIN`) — every one an `EvidencedInference` subtype or an
   `Analysis`, none an observation node. **`ASSIGNS` is one of the two edges §1's own diagram draws**,
   so the diagram contradicts its caption. Promoting `Contrast` would be a seventh breach of a clause
   already breached six times, which is an argument about direction and not a bright line.

   **What survives is not independent of ground 1.** All six existing breaches point at entities that
   exist outside this laboratory — a `Protein`, a `Modifier`, a `Publication`. `Contrast` would be the
   first reference node with no external authority behind it, which is ground 1 restated. **So option
   (ii) is rejected on two independent grounds, not three**, and either remains sufficient.

   **§1 l.26 is left standing and is not amended here.** It is a load-bearing sentence in a normative
   document; correcting it is a decision with its own record, not a review edit to this one.
3. **l.1079's own stated price**, which is not a scheduling problem. A contrast is a *pair* of
   locally-defined conditions; an external vocabulary might supply the components and none
   enumerates the pair.

**(v) Fully qualified strings — rejected.** It fixes the collision by convention, and a convention
is enforced by nothing. ADR-0021 forbids exactly this: an identifying field whose value depends on
curator discipline makes the id a function of who typed it. It also destroys cross-dataset matching
*by construction*, since fully qualified strings never repeat — paying (i)'s price without (i)'s
honesty about paying it.

**(vi) Split the node — rejected.** Architecturally it is the clean answer to *reference-like in
behaviour, evidence-like in origin*. Its reference half inherits (ii)'s blocker whole: the
definition node still needs a deterministic key over terms with no vocabulary. It buys a new node
type and a new edge to arrive at the same wall, and its cross-boundary edge meets objection 2 above.

**(iv) Accept the collision — rejected.** l.1081 establishes it fires on correct usage.

**(i) Per-dataset — rejected as a granularity, adopted as a shape.** Its outcome is right and its
scope is one level too fine, for the reasons under **Decision**. **The reasoning is the same
reasoning; only the anchored label differs.**

## What the chosen option costs

**1. Cross-dataset contrast matching, which is l.1079's named price and is real.** Two experiments
asking the same question get two nodes. *Which sites change in WT+IFN versus KO+IFN across all our
experiments* stops being a single-node lookup.

**2. And the loss is partial in a way worth stating precisely.** `numerator` and `denominator`
survive as properties, so the matching moves from the **id** to a **query** — a string join rather
than a node join. That is weaker, because nothing enforces canonical string form. **So option (ii)'s
cost does not disappear under this decision; it relocates.** The vocabulary that does not exist is
needed for reliable cross-experiment matching either way — as an identity input under (ii), as a
query-time normalisation here. The difference is that here its absence degrades a query, and there
it would have corrupted an id.

**3. A new edge is required, and this is a DDL change.** `Contrast` cannot be anchored on any
existing edge: its only edge is `RESULT_IN_CONTRAST`, which `DifferentialResult` already anchors
through at `schema.py` l.335, and anchoring `Contrast` back on `DifferentialResult` is a cycle —
`evidence_id` takes anchor **ids** as inputs, so neither could be computed first. ADR-0025's anchor
needed no DDL change because `ADJUSTED_BY` already existed; **this one is not free in the same way.**

**4. An ordering obligation, inherited from ADR-0025.** A `Contrast` must be keyed after its
`Experiment`.

**5. `DifferentialResult` ids become transitively dependent on `Experiment`**, since they anchor on
`Contrast` at l.335 and `Contrast` would anchor on `Experiment`.

**What cannot be established from this repository, and is therefore not estimated.** Whether an
external vocabulary for condition terms could be adopted or built — l.1079 records only that the
terms do not resolve to one today. And how often the anchor laboratory would want to match contrasts
across experiments, which decides how much cost 1 actually is. **Neither is in the repository and
neither is guessed at.**

## What this settles in practice — measured, not assumed

**No `Contrast` node is minted today.** Confirmed by loading `curation_PXD018299.json` through
`bzk/curation/loader.py` in memory: **16 nodes — 1 `Project`, 1 `Experiment`, 1 `Dataset`, 1
`Analysis`, 12 `Sample` — and 38 edges — 1 `CONTAINS`, 1 `USED`, 12 `PERFORMED_ON`, 12 `PRODUCED`,
12 `SAMPLE_GENERATED_BY`.** Zero `Contrast`, zero `DifferentialResult`, no `RESULT_IN_CONTRAST`.
loader.py l.357–359 says why, and names Q1 as the reason.

The two contrast strings are read and handed on: `KO_IFN_vs_WT_IFN` (`'USP18-/- + IFN'` over
`'WT + IFN'`) and `KO_vs_WT_unstimulated`, with `analysis_PXD018299_KOIFN_vs_WTIFN.json` naming the
first.

**What the loader would do differently.** Materialise a `Contrast` node per entry in
`contrasts_of_interest` — two for `PXD018299` — mint the new edge from each to the `Experiment`, key
them with `{"Experiment": experiment_id}` as the anchor, and lose the l.357–358 comment. **Described,
not made.**

**Which currently mintable ids change: none.** Established rather than assumed — of the five labels
the loader mints, `Project` and `Dataset` have no anchors, `Experiment` anchors on `Project`,
`Analysis` on `Dataset`, and `Sample` on `Experiment`. **Not one references `Contrast`, directly or
transitively.** So the three pinned literals do **not** move:

| Literal | Holds | Moves? |
|---|---|---|
| `tests/fixtures/pxd018299_curation_ids.json` | project, experiment, dataset, analysis, twelve samples | **no** |
| `HANDOFF.md` l.504 | the `Analysis` id | **no** |
| `tests/test_perseus.py` l.74 | `curation_analysis_id` | **no** |

**`DifferentialResult` ids: none exists to change.** No producer constructs one — the only
`evidence_id("DifferentialResult", …)` calls in `bzk/` are in `invariants.py` l.646 and l.652, where
the validator re-derives an id to check it. **Forward-looking, and stated as such:** once such nodes
are minted, their ids will depend on the `Contrast` id and hence on the `Experiment`.

**So this decision moves no id that exists.** It fixes what future ids will be, which is why it is
recordable now and why nothing needs a rebuild to record it.

## Implied changes, described and not made

1. **`ONTOLOGY.md` §4/§5 DDL gains the edge** — shape `CREATE REL TABLE CONTRAST_IN_EXPERIMENT(FROM
   Contrast TO Experiment, MANY_ONE);`. The direction is not arbitrary: `invariants.py` l.632–635
   reads `edge["from"]` as the anchored node and `edge["to"]` as the anchor, so an edge from
   `Contrast` to `Experiment` matches the idiom that path already checks.
2. **`Contrast` joins §1's partition diagram, evidence column.**
3. **`schema.py` l.274 gains `anchors=(("Experiment", "CONTRAST_IN_EXPERIMENT"),)`, and l.593's
   block gains the `RelTable`.** Neither is made here, and the reason is checkable rather than
   stylistic: `tests/test_schema.py::test_identity_table_matches_ddl` asserts at l.242 that every
   edge cited in §3's anchors cell is a declared rel table, and
   `test_schema_identity_matches_ontology_table` compares §3's anchors against `schema.IDENTITY` in
   both directions. **Writing the anchor into §3 today would turn the suite red.** So §3's row
   records the settlement in prose and cites no anchor, which keeps document and code in step while
   the decision is `Proposed`.
4. **`bzk/curation/loader.py` materialises `Contrast`**, per the paragraph above.
5. **A guard that `Contrast` carries an anchor**, once the edge exists — the assertion that would
   have caught l.1081 before it was found by audit.
6. **No invariant is added or amended here**, and none of I6, I8 or I9 is touched.
